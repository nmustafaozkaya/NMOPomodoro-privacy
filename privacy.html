Focusdoro Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası

Last Updated / Son Güncelleme: 09 June 2026

English

Focusdoro respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information.

Information We Collect

Focusdoro does not collect, store, or share personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location data
  • Personal identification information

Local Data Storage

Focusdoro may store productivity-related information such as completed sessions, timer settings, and focus statistics locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to our servers.

Third-Party Services

Focusdoro does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services to collect user data.

The application may rely on Google Play services for distribution and updates. Please review Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Security

Since Focusdoro does not collect personal information, user data remains on the device and is not shared with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Focusdoro does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact
